Unsure? Let's Talk

Not sure if it's a real gap? Here's how to validate before submitting a proposal.

Bring Question or Issue to S&T

A Figma file, prototype etc. applicable, along with the topic or concern. This gives the DS team context before the session.

Book a DS Office Hour

Book a office hours session with the DS team. Bring unresolved questions or component concerns that didn't make it into S&T weekly. No prep needed.

Post Your Proposal or Question

Make a request in #design-system on Slack. We will review it and get back to you. Expect an answer or next steps shortly after.

Submit a Request

Designers: What to Include

A Figma frame of the problem or concept, the affected use cases and flows, and why existing components fall short.

Developers: What to Include

A code snippet if you have one, screenshots of the issue, and the impact on your team or feature.

Was Your Request Accepted?

Request Not Accepted. Now What?

A rejection means not yet, not never. The steps below help you understand why and come back with a stronger case.

Understand Why It Was Declined

Ask the DS team for specific feedback. Was it scope, timing, precedent, or an existing solution that already covers the need?

Update Your Request and Resubmit

A declined request is not final. Review why it was declined, add stronger context, and bring it back to #design-system.

Build Locally as a Last Resort

This is your last option, not a shortcut. Use DS tokens and patterns, document everything, and plan to replace it.

Would Other Teams Need It?

If at least 2 other teams would use it, it belongs in the design system.

How your component gets contributed to Team Gaia's library.

Align with Team Gaia

Work with Team Gaia to align on design tokens and API surface.

Submit for Review

Submit a design review in Figma and a code implementation for review.

Ship in the Library

Once accepted, it ships in the LeanIX component library — credit to your team.

Build It Locally but Future-Friendly

No shared component exists yet. This is the exception, not the rule. Follow these steps to keep the door open for contribution later.

Name It Clearly

Name it local and temporary. If your team doesn't know its status, it will be treated as permanent.

Follow DS Patterns

Use design tokens and UI5 API patterns. No custom styles, no shortcuts. It must be replaceable.

Document the Why

Document the use case and why it's not shared yet. If you skip this, the next team will build it again.
